The solution

Don't change what you use. Change when.

Best window for every appliance
Live grid data finds the cheapest, cleanest slot and shows the € and CO2 saved against your usual time.
Cost, carbon or both
You choose what matters. The windows move with it.
Honest on a flat rate
Timing saves €0 on a flat plan, and we say so. Then we show what a smart plan would save you.
EV, solar and battery homes
Charging windows that meet a deadline, and a solar year showing when the grid pays you.
Ask in plain English
"My car needs 80% by 8am" gets a plan from the same live data.

Data

Built on Irish public data, live

EirGrid Smart Grid Dashboard LIVE · 15 MIN

Grid CO2 intensity (gCO2/kWh) and the 36-hour wind forecast. EirGrid publishes no CO2 forecast, so we fit one from the wind forecast and label it an estimate.

Met Éireann open forecast API LIVE · HOURLY

Solar radiation (W/m²) for the next 48 hours from the HARMONIE model, turned into each home's solar output.

PVGIS, EU Joint Research Centre LIVE

Monthly solar output for any location, roof tilt and direction. Powers the solar year and the export credit.

Tariffs and households SAMPLE · SYNTHETIC

Flat, smart time-of-use and EV tariff structures with sample rates, and synthetic household profiles. Next: real rates read from a photo of your bill.

Every number on screen is labelled as data, estimate or AI. If a feed fails, the app falls back to a dated sample and says so.
