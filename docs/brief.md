## Challenge

"Use data and AI to make everyday life in Ireland better." Irish public data
at the heart, plus own or permissioned data where useful. Start with one
neighbourhood, business or everyday task. Get one compelling demo working.

## Submission

Project link, team names, data sources. Relaxed showcase: no judging panel, no
cash prize.

Demo: who it helps and their problem, the working prototype on one example,
the data used, what you'd improve next.

## Rules of thumb from the organisers

- Prove the data works before polishing the interface.
- Show sources and update dates. Separate data-backed facts from AI suggestions.
- Keep a dated sample as a demo fallback if a live feed fails.
- Credit data providers and follow their usage limits.
- Label synthetic data and unverified reports clearly. Show missing evidence.
- Keep API keys on the server, out of shared code.
- Smart-meter data (ESB Networks) is personal: use your own export,
  permissioned data, or a synthetic sample.
